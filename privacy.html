Privacy Policy for Parlor Checkers

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Data stored on your device

Your settings, statistics and unfinished game are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded, and the app has no account or login.

Advertising

This app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, you can change your ad consent under Privacy Choices in the app's Settings, and turn off tracking under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13.
